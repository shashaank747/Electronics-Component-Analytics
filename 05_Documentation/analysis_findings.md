# Analysis Findings

## Overview
This document compiles the analytical findings generated by the **Power BI Data Model** and **DAX Measures** for the **Electronics Component Inventory and Procurement Analysis Dashboard**.

---

## 1. Inventory Analytics Findings

### Finding 1: Catalog Scale & Category Composition
- The catalog contains **105 distinct electronic components** across 8 standardized categories.
- **Microcontrollers and Development Boards** (`CAT006`) and **Sensors** (`CAT005`) hold the largest share of high-complexity items (15 components each), while **Passive Components** (`CAT001`) account for the highest volume of individual units.

### Finding 2: Inventory Holding Valuation & Capital Concentration
- Total estimated inventory valuation fluctuates around **₹4.8M to ₹5.6M** across monthly snapshot periods.
- High-value categories:
  1. `Microcontrollers and Development Boards`: Represents ~42% of total inventory valuation despite comprising only ~14% of catalog part numbers.
  2. `Sensors`: Represents ~24% of inventory holding value.
  3. `Integrated Circuits`: Represents ~16% of inventory value.
- Lower-cost categories (Passives, Diodes/LEDs, Connectors) represent less than 10% of holding value combined, despite accounting for ~65% of physical piece count.

### Finding 3: Stockout and Shortage Vulnerabilities
- Across the 12 monthly observation dates (1,260 observations), **24 zero-stock events** were recorded (averaging ~2 components per month).
- An average of **9 to 11 components per month** dipped below safety reorder levels, primarily driven by lead-time delays in semiconductor shipments.

### Finding 4: Rental Asset Fleet
- **16 components** (principally development platforms like Arduino Mega, STM32 kits, and advanced sensor modules) are marked as **Rental Eligible**, presenting an alternate recurring revenue stream without depleting core production inventory.

## 2. Procurement & Supplier Analytics Findings

### Finding 1: Purchase Order Fulfillment Distribution
- Across 2025, **100 total purchase orders** were issued:
  - **Delivered**: 80 orders (80.0%)
  - **Pending**: 12 orders (12.0%) — concentrated in late Q4 2025.
  - **Cancelled**: 8 orders (8.0%) — attributable to supplier stockouts or obsolete revisions.

### Finding 2: Total Procurement Spend & Concentration
- Net committed procurement expenditure across non-cancelled purchase orders totals approximately **₹2.4M**.
- Spend concentration:
  - Top 10 suppliers account for ~45% of total procurement spend.
  - Top vendor partners include `ElectroVibe Components Pvt Ltd` (`SUP001`), `Apex Micro Devices India` (`SUP002`), and `Silicon Bridge Technologies` (`SUP003`).

### Finding 3: Delivery Lead Time & Supplier Reliability
- The overall **average delivery lead time** for delivered orders is **11.4 days** (ranging from 5 to 22 days).
- **On-Time Delivery Rate (OTD %)** stands at **76.25%** (61 out of 80 delivered orders arrived on or before expected delivery date).
- 19 orders experienced minor delays (averaging 2.8 days of delay), primarily during high-demand procurement windows in Q2 and Q3.

### Finding 4: Supplier Purchase Price Variance
- Negotiated line-item purchase prices varied by **$\pm 3\%$ to $\pm 12\%$** relative to standard benchmark catalog costs.
- Bulk procurement orders of passive components achieved volume discounts averaging 8.5%, whereas spot buys for specialized microcontroller breakout boards saw cost premiums of up to 11.2%.
