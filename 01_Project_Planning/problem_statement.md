# Problem Statement

## Context
Electronics manufacturing and distribution businesses handle an extensive and diverse catalog of components, including:
- Passive Components (resistors, capacitors, inductors)
- Discrete Semiconductors (diodes, LEDs, transistors, MOSFETs)
- Integrated Circuits (analog, digital, power management ICs)
- Sensors (temperature, optical, pressure, motion)
- Microcontrollers & Development Boards
- Connectors, Cables, and Electromechanical parts

Managing these components requires continuous oversight across multiple operational variables: stock quantities, reorder thresholds, component costs, inventory conditions, and ongoing purchasing activities.

## The Challenge
When inventory and procurement data is maintained across disconnected spreadsheets or disparate records, understanding operational stock health and procurement performance becomes time-consuming:
- **Stockout & Shortage Risks**: Identifying low-stock or zero-stock components requires manual comparison against reorder levels, leading to stockouts or line stoppages.
- **Inaccurate Valuation**: Estimating total inventory holding value across categories is difficult to assess dynamically.
- **Uncontrolled Procurement Costs**: Comparing purchase price variance across different suppliers for identical parts is cumbersome.
- **Supplier Delivery Delays**: Monitoring vendor lead times, pending orders, and delivery delays demands manual tracking across scattered order logs.

## Proposed Solution
The project proposes an integrated Data Analytics and Business Intelligence solution that organizes inventory and procurement information into structured relational datasets, analyzes it using SQL and Python, and presents actionable insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
